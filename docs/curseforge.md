## Summary

Enemy nameplates only while you're in combat. Nothing to configure.

## Description

Dynamic Display Nameplate shows enemy nameplates when you enter combat and hides them when you leave. It's the "combat plates" behavior from Leatrix Plus, on its own, for World of Warcraft: Forever.

**How it works**

- Entering combat turns enemy nameplates on.
- Leaving combat turns them off.
- Logging in, reloading, or zoning out of combat turns them off.

There are no options, slash commands, or saved settings. Install it and it works.

**Good to know**

- If you press V to show nameplates outside combat, they will hide again at the end of your next fight.
- The addon changes only the enemy nameplate setting. Friendly nameplates and "Always show nameplates" are left as you set them.
- If you uninstall it, enemy nameplates stay off. Press V to turn them back on.

**Compatibility**

- World of Warcraft: Forever (1.60.x)
- Works alongside other nameplate addons. It only decides when enemy nameplates are shown, not what they look like.
